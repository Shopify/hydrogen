Calibration on our own template

Healthy stores

8/8 PASS, including the cart-page-only and popup variants that the scripted test failed.

Broken stores

8/8 hard faults caught as BLOCKED in under a minute. "Quantity ignored" came out as PASS with friction, because a shopper can fix it.

Cost

$0.10–0.40 per agent run. Replay scripts cost $0 and take 2–9 s.
